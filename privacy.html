Privacy policy

Queue for Apple TV · Last updated 25 October 2026

Queue is a personal Apple TV app that manages your YouTube playlists. This policy explains what it does with your information. The short version: everything stays on your Apple TV or in your own YouTube account, and the developer never receives any of it.

What Queue accesses

When you sign in with Google, Queue asks for the YouTube permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). With it, Queue reads your playlists and the videos in them, and makes the changes you ask for: adding, removing, moving and reordering videos, and creating playlists. It also sends your searches to YouTube to get results and search suggestions.

Queue does not ask for your email address, contacts, or any other Google data.

Where your information is kept

  • Your sign-in (the Google refresh token) is stored in the Apple TV's Keychain.
  • App data such as which playlists are your Queue and Saved, recent searches, your topic rows, a list of recently watched videos, a count of today's YouTube API usage, and cached copies of your playlists is stored on the Apple TV in the app's own storage.
  • Your playlists live in your YouTube account, as they always have.

Queue has no server. It talks only to Google: Google's sign-in service, the YouTube Data API, YouTube's search suggestions, and YouTube's image servers for video thumbnails. Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else.

What Queue does not do

  • It does not sell, share or transfer your information to anyone.
  • It does not use your information for advertising, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not use your information for any purpose other than showing and changing your playlists in the app.

Google and YouTube

Queue uses YouTube API Services. By using Queue you are also using YouTube, so the YouTube Terms of Service apply, and Google handles your data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Queue's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your information

  • Sign out in Queue's Settings. This deletes the sign-in from the Apple TV and asks Google to revoke it. If the Apple TV is offline at that moment, Google may not hear about it, so also revoke access as described below if you want to be sure.
  • Delete the app from the Apple TV to remove the app data it stored there. The Apple TV's Keychain can keep the sign-in after the app is deleted, so sign out first (or revoke access below).
  • Revoke access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
