Queue for Apple TV

A personal Apple TV app for managing your own YouTube playlists.

Queue is a small tvOS app built by one person for their own Apple TV. It is not sold or listed on the App Store; it is installed directly from Xcode.

What it does

  • Keeps a Queue (videos to watch soon) and Saved (videos to watch eventually) as ordinary private playlists in your YouTube account, plus any other playlists you have.
  • Lets you move, reorder, remove and mark videos watched, one at a time or several at once.
  • Searches YouTube and builds "topic" rows from channels or searches you choose.
  • Hands playback to the official YouTube app. Queue never plays video itself.

Why it asks for access to your YouTube account

You sign in with Google on your phone using a code shown on the TV. Queue asks for the YouTube permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube) because it needs to read your playlists and change them: add videos, remove them, reorder them and create playlists. It uses that access only to do what you ask in the app.

Queue uses YouTube API Services. Your sign-in stays on your Apple TV, and nothing is sent to any server run by the developer. The privacy policy has the details.
